# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SinterDB is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in its projects and community spaces.

We welcome contributors and users of all experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate
- Keep discussions constructive and focused
- Give and receive technical feedback professionally
- Respect differing opinions and approaches
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory behavior
- Help maintain a collaborative environment
- Assume good faith where reasonable

Disagreement is normal in software development. Critique ideas, implementations, and decisions without attacking the people behind them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal insults or targeted hostility
- Threats or encouragement of violence
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, issues, pull requests, or other project spaces
- Repeatedly ignoring reasonable requests from maintainers
- Abusive, malicious, or intentionally destructive behavior toward contributors or users

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to SinterDB organization spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Code reviews
- Other official SinterDB community spaces

It may also apply when someone is representing SinterDB publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, responses may include:

- A private warning
- Removal of comments or other content
- Temporary restrictions
- Closing or locking discussions, issues, or pull requests
- Temporary or permanent removal from SinterDB community spaces

Actions will be taken based on the situation and available evidence.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the SinterDB maintainers privately when possible.

Do not use public issues to report sensitive conduct concerns.

Reports should include any relevant context, links, screenshots, or other information that may help the maintainers understand what occurred.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as reasonably possible.

## Good-Faith Participation

This Code of Conduct is intended to protect healthy collaboration, not discourage reasonable disagreement, criticism, or technical debate.

Good-faith mistakes can usually be resolved through communication and correction.

Repeated, deliberate, or severe misconduct may result in stronger action.
